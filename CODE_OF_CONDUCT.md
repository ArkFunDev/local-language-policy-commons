# Code of Conduct

This project serves communities whose languages and knowledge have often been overlooked by technology. We expect everyone who participates — in issues, pull requests, discussions and the application itself — to help keep it respectful and safe.

## Our standards

Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are not fluent in the language of a discussion. Welcome people of every background, language, gender, age, ability, ethnicity, religion and experience level. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Credit other people's work. Treat language communities as the authorities on their own languages and cultures.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, ridiculing someone's language or accent, publishing other people's private information, sexualised or violent content, sustained disruption, and submitting content designed to mislead or to target individuals or groups.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately through GitHub (use *Report content* on the item, or contact the maintainer through the profile linked in the README). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation, when behaviour violates these standards.

## Attribution

This code of conduct follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) version 2.1, adapted for a multilingual public-interest project.
